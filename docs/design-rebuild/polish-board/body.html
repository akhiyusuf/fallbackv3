What is missing outside Today

  1. No screen but Today has a headline. Routines, Events, Courses and To-dos open straight into a list. Nothing tells you how you are doing there.
  2. Nothing looks touchable except buttons. Cards are flat with a hairline border, so a card you can open looks the same as a panel you can't.
  3. Look-alikes that do nothing. The green and blue plan tiles on Routines and Courses look like Today's Done and Fallback buttons, but they are only labels.
  4. Progress is written, not drawn. Levels, XP and history are mostly sentences and thin bars. Locked badges are identical grey circles.
  5. Empty screens are empty. Events with nothing today, Notifications and Records leave most of the screen blank.
  6. The type has no character and nothing moves. One plain typeface at similar weights everywhere, and logging gives no physical feedback.

What "polished" means here

The apps you are thinking of share five habits. None of them needs a new colour: your themes, the green, the blue and the gold stay exactly as approved.

  1. Things look pressable. Chunky shapes with a visible edge.
  2. Every screen has one headline. A number or a state, in the theme colour.
  3. Type with character. A rounded face and heavy numbers.
  4. Progress is drawn. Rings, thick bars, week dots, medallions.
  5. The app reacts. Buttons press down, ticks pop, bars fill.

Each decision below applies one of these to a different part of the app. The phone lets you flip between seven screens to see them together.
