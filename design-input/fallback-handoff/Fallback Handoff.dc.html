Screen design handoff

Fallback

A phones-only habit tracker built on one idea — every habit has an ideal and a fallback, and a fallback still counts. This document lays out the app's 50 screens as a developer reference: each frame is labelled with its screen ID, route, and state, and the tab bar, back controls and primary CTAs link between frames so you can trace a flow. Built on the Verdant design system.

Accent = Forge Orange

Per your note, the accent is Forge Orange — not Verdant's leaf green. It recolours CTAs, progress, the active tab and the add button, and nothing else. Ideal, fallback and off-day keep their own signal hues.

State colours are load-bearing

Ideal (green), fallback (blue) and off (grey) are semantic, never decoration. Missed is only ever the unfilled remainder of a bar — never a named colour, matching the no-streak framing.

Custom components, built to token

Widgets Verdant doesn't ship (StateChip, ConsistencyBreakdownBar, BottomTabs, OffDayToggle…) are composed from Verdant tokens and shown inline — geometry and shadows follow the system.

Fixture data is spec-exact

Copy and numbers (Maya, 84%, 26 of 31 days, the sample week) are verbatim from the spec so engineering can match strings and math directly.
